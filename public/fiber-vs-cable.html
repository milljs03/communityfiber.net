Fiber vs Cable Internet

Discover why fiber internet is the superior choice for speed, reliability, and future-proof connectivity

Feature Fiber Internet Cable Internet
Download Speed Up to 1 Gbps Up to 400 Mbps
Upload Speed Up to 1 Gbps Up to 20 Mbps
Latency (Ping) 10-20ms (excellent) 20-50ms (good)
Reliability 99.9%+ uptime 95-98% uptime
Data Caps No data caps Often limited (500GB-1TB)
Symmetrical Speed Equal upload/download Asymmetrical (upload much slower)
Network Congestion Dedicated connection Shared bandwidth with neighbors
Future-Proof Ready for 10+ Gbps Limited upgrade potential
Weather Impact Minimal impact Can degrade in storms
Contracts No long-term contracts Often 2-year contracts
