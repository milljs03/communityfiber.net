Mobile questions

Do I have to be an NPTech internet customer?
Yes, and Community Fiber counts. NPTech is the parent company and Community Fiber is its internet service, so every Community Fiber subscriber already has an active NPTech broadband account. If you do not have service yet, check availability at your address and we can talk about mobile once your internet is installed.
Can I keep my current phone number?
Yes. Number transfers are part of a normal switch. Keep your existing service active until the transfer completes so you are never without a working phone.
Can I use the phone I already own?
In most cases, yes, provided the phone is unlocked and compatible with our network. Get in touch with your phone's model and we will confirm before anything changes.
How many lines can I add?
Multiple lines can be added to a single NPTech account. Contact us for the current limit and for pricing on additional lines.
What happens to my mobile service if I cancel my internet?
An active NPTech broadband account, Community Fiber included, is required to keep mobile lines, because mobile is offered only to broadband subscribers. Talk to us before making changes and we will explain your options.

Tell us how many lines you need and which phones you want to keep. We will confirm compatibility and current pricing.
