What internet speeds can I get in Nappanee?

Community Fiber offers symmetrical plans from 100 Mbps up to 1 gigabit (1,000 Mbps) in Nappanee. Symmetrical means your upload speed matches your download speed — something cable cannot do — so video calls, cloud backups, and gaming feel just as fast as streaming.
