What Internet Speed Do I Need?

Match your household's streaming, gaming, work, school, and smart home needs to the right fiber internet speed.

1 min read Updated 2026-06-25

Choosing an internet plan is easier when you think about how your home actually uses the connection. A small household that streams shows and checks email needs something different than a busy home with remote work, online classes, gaming, security cameras, and large downloads happening at the same time.

Quick answer

  • Around 100 Mbps covers everyday browsing, email, and streaming on a few devices — a good fit for smaller households.
  • Around 200 Mbps suits busier homes with schoolwork, several streams, and a few connected devices.
  • Around 500 Mbps gives families more room for video calls, streaming, smart TVs, tablets, and connected devices.
  • 1 Gig is best for heavy-use homes, gaming, large downloads, 4K streaming, creators, and households where many devices are active at once.

Why fiber speed feels different

Fiber internet is not only about download speed. Every Community Fiber plan is symmetrical — your upload speed matches your download speed. That matters for video calls, sending files, cloud backups, security cameras, and working from home. That upload side is one reason fiber often feels smoother than older cable connections.

Think about simultaneous use

The right plan depends less on one device and more on how many things are happening at the same time. One 4K stream may not be a problem, but a 4K stream, a video call, online gaming, phone updates, and cloud backups can stack up quickly.

Local tip

If your household has grown into more streaming, remote work, or smart home devices over the last few years, choose the plan that gives you room to grow instead of only matching today's minimum need.

When to choose the fastest plan

The top speed tier makes the most sense when the internet is central to daily life. If multiple people are streaming, gaming, working, uploading files, and using smart devices at the same time, the extra room can reduce bottlenecks and make the connection feel more consistent.
